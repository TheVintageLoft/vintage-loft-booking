6 · Cancellations

One red Cancel button opens three choices:

1 · With credit Outside the window · 48 hours, or 7 days on Christmas weekends Their payment becomes account credit · they get an email
2 · Without credit Inside the window · too close to their start time No credit · a gentle policy email is sent
3 · Quiet cancel Never paid, never told · pencil-ins and inquiries No email at all · nothing to credit
