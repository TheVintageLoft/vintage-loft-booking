Staff App Training

Welcome to training

Six short hands-on modules on a practice copy of the Staff App. Nothing here touches real bookings, so tap everything freely. Each module ends with a checkmark; finish all six and your completion card appears.

0 of 6 complete

1 · Signing in

The Staff App lives at /hold.html and is installed on the studio phone and iPad as an app. You sign in once with the staff code and stay signed in for 30 days. Kelly will give you the real code in person; it is never written down in emails or texts.

YOUR TASK: sign in to the practice app with the practice code training
✓Signed in. This is exactly how the real app works.

2 · Reading the schedule

The calendar shows every studio's day. Learn the colours: Grand red, Dream blue, Gatsby green, Carnegie brown, Marilyn purple.

Three things to spot on any entry: the Booking or Hold tag, the green PAID stamp in the corner (paid, Vintage Films, and comps all count as paid), and slim Setup cards, which are reserved early-arrival time that belongs to the booking above them and can't be edited on their own.

YOUR TASK: one of these bookings has not been paid. Tap it.
✓Right. No PAID stamp means money is still owed. Always glance for the stamp.

3 · Find a client

Search any name to see a client's bookings, contact info, account credit, and the standing note about them. Notes are collapsed to keep things tidy: tap the header to open them.

YOUR TASK: search for Sarah, open her note, and type her account credit below.
✓Found it. Credit shows on every client, but only Kelly can change it. Cancellation credits are automatic.

4 · Adding a booking

Phone bookings go in through Add to the calendar. Pick the studio, date, and time, add any add-ons, and leave Notify client ticked so they get the email with their payment link. Use + Add another studio for a second same-day studio; a different day should be its own booking.

YOUR TASK: book the Gatsby studio for 2 hours, add the Pet fee, keep Notify client ticked, then Add to calendar.
✓Booked. The client gets one email with everything, including the payment link.

5 · Payments

Your booking from module 4 is unpaid. The colour-coded buttons: Mark as paid for money received (card, e-transfer, cash), Create payment link to send a pay-online link, and Vintage Films when Kelly's own team is using the studio: full value gets tallied, nobody is charged. Comps are Kelly-only, so you won't see that button.

Priya N Booking
Gatsby Room · 2 hours · Pet fee
YOUR TASK: Priya paid by e-transfer this morning. Do the right thing.
✓Marked paid. The PAID stamp now shows on the calendar, and her receipt reflects it.

6 · Cancellations

One red Cancel button, three choices. With credit: outside the window (48 hours, or 7 days on Christmas weekends), their payment becomes account credit and they get an email. Without credit: inside the window, no credit, a gentle policy email. Quiet cancel: no email at all, for bookings never paid or never confirmed to the client.
